## 📖 Welcome to the Storyfold

What if the fairy tales you grew up with were still happening—and someone had begun stealing their endings?

One strange bell rings thirteen times in a world where it should ring only twelve. A mysterious bookshop opens its door. Beyond its shelves are kingdoms filled with familiar faces, hidden roads, enchanted clocks, forgotten promises, and stories that have started to go wrong.

**You are the newest adventurer in the Storyfold.**

With the help of Mistress Quill, a wise and secretive bookshop keeper, and Pip, a tiny mouse with enormous opinions, you begin investigating what the extra chime means. Before long, you cross paths with Red Riding Hood, the Wolf, Jack, Cinderella, Hansel, Gretel, and other characters who are much more than the stories people tell about them.

But the deeper you travel, the stranger the mystery becomes. A lantern keeper has vanished. A whole day is missing from one kingdom. Midnight refuses to end in another. Records disagree. Witnesses are afraid to speak. Somewhere beyond the last page, someone believes that every story would be safer if nobody could choose how it ends.

**Your choices will decide whom you trust, which clues you find, what you uncover, and which future you help protect.**

This is not a story you simply read from beginning to end. It is one you *play*—and the next time you begin, a different adventure may be waiting.

> **The heart of the game:** A famous ending is not the only ending. Every person deserves the chance to choose their own story.

| Your starting adventure | What awaits you |
| --- | --- |

| Chapter | The adventure, without major spoilers |

## 🧙 Meet Familiar Characters in New Ways

The game draws on classic, public-domain fairy tales while giving their characters individual personalities, goals, fears, humor, and freedom to make their own decisions.

- **Red Riding Hood** is brave, observant, and more than the girl with the red cloak.
- **The Wolf** wants to be heard fairly instead of judged by his reputation.
- **Jack** has plenty of courage—and sometimes needs reminding to look before he leaps.
- **Cinderella** knows the royal palace holds more secrets than its grandest ballroom.
- **Hansel and Gretel** bring different talents to their investigation of Gingerbread March.
- **Mistress Quill** knows the Midnight Bookshop, its hidden shelves, and more history than she first admits.
- **Pip** is a small mouse with a big notebook, an even bigger personality, and a talent for asking exactly the wrong question at exactly the right time.

Other friends, rivals, witnesses, and unexpected allies appear as the mystery unfolds.

**Being someone's friend does not automatically mean they agree with you.** How you listen, help, apologize, and keep your promises affects your relationships and the adventures ahead.

## 🎮 How the Game Works

### Read, choose, and discover

The story is presented in illustrated scenes with readable text and large, touch-friendly buttons. You decide what to say, where to go, whom to help, which clues to investigate, and how to handle difficult choices.

Your decisions can affect:

- **The story:** different conversations, investigations, and scene branches.
- **Your friendships:** how companions respond and whether they want to help.
- **The evidence:** what you find, what you can prove, and what you still need to check.
- **Your character:** which skills you improve and how you solve problems.
- **The ending:** which future your adventure can earn.
- **Your next playthrough:** new paths and mysteries that weren't available the first time.

Some decisions have small, immediate consequences. Others return many chapters later. The **Journal** and **Consequence Ledger** help you remember what happened.

### Solve mysteries, not just quizzes

The game includes hands-on, tap-friendly activities alongside its story choices. You can examine objects, compare clues, arrange pages, follow paths, separate facts from guesses, and work with enchanted mechanisms.

**There are 26 interactive mini-games and puzzles**, with multiple kinds of activities throughout the kingdoms. Mistakes are part of learning: puzzles have help options and do not demand fast reactions or perfect timing.

### Grow as a LitRPG adventurer

Choose a play style that fits the way you like to solve problems:

| Class | Best at |
| --- | --- |
| **Trail Scout** | Courage, exploration, and finding safe ways through danger. |
| **Archive Sleuth** | Wit, discovering clues, and noticing details other people miss. |
| **Story Guardian** | Heart, listening, helping friends, and keeping people safe. |

Earn **experience points**, reach up to **Level 20**, improve **Courage, Wit, and Heart**, and unlock **nine talents**. Thirty additional skill encounters offer different approaches to challenges.

Companions also have their own specialties. Their assistance depends on the story and their willingness—not just a high number on a friendship meter. Every class has a path forward, and a failed skill check won't trap you in an unwinnable game.

### Keep your adventure organized

Use the in-game tools to track your journey:

- **Map:** kingdoms and the roads between them.
- **Journal:** current quests, people, discoveries, and important decisions.
- **Case Board:** evidence, possible explanations, and what has actually been proved.
- **Satchel / Inventory:** collected items and helpful story objects.
- **Character Build:** class, level, abilities, and attributes.
- **Companions:** relationships and teamwork abilities.
- **Living Atlas:** completed endings and rewards from later adventures.

## 🔁 Your First Ending Is Only the Beginning

Completing Book One unlocks **New Game Plus** through the *Living Atlas*.

Your first completed ending is not erased. It remains part of your collection of possible histories. A new telling lets you make different choices, follow other characters, try another class, and discover things your first adventurer never saw.

Replay features include:

- **12 distinct ending families**, with different conditions, consequences, and epilogues.
- **Reflection Road**, an additional starting adventure unlocked after completing a telling.
- **25 replay-exclusive side adventures**, including mysteries unlocked across later Legacy tiers.
- **Class-specific investigations** for Scouts, Sleuths, and Guardians.
- **Story Sparks** and optional **Living Atlas** rewards earned by completing different replay adventures.
- **New dialogue and callbacks** that reflect discoveries from previous tellings—without magically giving you proof you haven't found in the current story.

Replay unlocks are earned by playing. Not every path, secret, or ending is available in a single run.

## 💛 Built for Young Readers and Families

**Recommended reading audience: ages 8–12.** Older fantasy fans are welcome too!

The game aims for the adventure, humor, mystery, heart, and easy-to-follow storytelling of middle-grade fantasy. Difficult choices have real consequences, but the presentation stays age-appropriate.

- **No timed puzzles, lost lives, or harsh game-over punishment for wrong answers.**
- **Hints and gentle recovery paths** help players keep going.
- **Large buttons and portrait-first screens** support one-handed phone play.
- **Adjustable text, reduced motion, and optional audio** let readers choose a comfortable experience.
- **Audio begins muted**; listening is never required to solve a puzzle.
- **No account, chat, advertising, or in-game purchases** are bundled in this build.
- **Local autosaving and backup export/import** support continuing an adventure later.

**Content note:** Expect mild fairy-tale peril, mysterious villains, false accusations, and moments when characters must make difficult moral decisions. The story avoids graphic violence and is designed for a middle-grade audience.
