Privacy for Families

You're in charge of your story.

This game does not ask you to make an account. You can choose a made-up character name.

Your story progress, choices, and settings are saved in this browser on this device. You can export a save file if you want a backup.

This version does not contain advertising, analytics trackers, chat, or in-game purchases. The game's own code does not send your hero name or story choices to a game server.

When you visit the website, the web host may receive technical information needed to deliver the pages, such as your IP address. The game can also store offline copies of artwork, music, and code on your device.

You can remove your progress by clearing this site's browser data. If you export a backup, keep it private; it contains your saved story and name.

Parents and guardians: this page describes the code included in this release. The person publishing this game must review their web host, domain, contact details, and privacy obligations before a public launch.
